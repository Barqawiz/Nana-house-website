A home that ages

Ten chapters, one house. Each room is built carefully to feel like its own moment in time, with the light and the furniture changing as the years pass.

📦

Objects remember

A teapot that keeps appearing. A toy that moves rooms. A chair that empties out. Nothing is explained. You'll understand anyway.

🧩

Quiet exercise for the mind

Read the room, sort things by kind and by owner, and reason out where each piece belongs. A real workout for the mind, with no timer chasing you.

🤲

Made to be felt

Every object has its own designed haptic. Things settle, click, and land differently depending on what they are. Play with the sound off and you'll still feel the room.

🗣️

Every object narrates

Turn on VoiceOver and each object tells you its own memory, a second, complete way to experience the whole story.

🌍

English and Arabic

Full right-to-left support, with the house, the objects, and the memories written for both languages from the start.
