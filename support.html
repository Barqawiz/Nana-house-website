Nana's House

Support

A quiet story game by IntelliNode.

Need help or have feedback? Email intellinode.comp@gmail.com and we'll get back to you, usually within 1 to 2 business days.
How do I play?
Open the box, then drag each object to the gold‑dashed outline where it belongs. An object only settles, with a haptic click, when it's in the right spot. There's no timer and no way to fail a placement; it just won't settle until it's right.
What if I get stuck?
Tap the hint bulb. Each chapter gives you a small budget of hints that briefly highlight where an object belongs, so you're never stuck for long.
Is the game free?
Chapters 1 and 2 are completely free. A single $7.99 purchase unlocks chapters 3 through 10 forever. There are no ads, no coins, no subscriptions, and no timers anywhere in the game.
Do I need an account or an internet connection?
No. Nana's House needs no account and plays fully offline. Game Center (for achievements) and iCloud sync are optional and are the only features that use a connection.
How do I restore my purchase?
Open Settings from the home screen and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the Apple Account used for the original purchase.
What languages does it support?
English and more languages, with complete right‑to‑left support built in. Switch languages any time from Settings.
Does it support VoiceOver?
Yes. Every object in every room narrates its own memory when focused with VoiceOver, a full second way to experience the story.
